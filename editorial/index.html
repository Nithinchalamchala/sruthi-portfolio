About Sruthi

Curious by default.
Careful by habit.

I grew up in Kandukur, finished school and junior college with top marks, and in 2024 began a five-year Integrated M.Tech in Computer Science at VIT-AP University. Three years in, what I enjoy most is turning a messy real-world problem into a system that simply works.

I want to grow into a software engineer who works where computer science meets everyday life, especially healthcare and public safety. Right now I'm looking for a software or AI/ML internship where I can ship real features, learn from experienced engineers, and keep getting better at writing clean, reliable code.

I like proving that the quiet person in the lab can be the one who finds the bug. I help juniors in my hostel who are new to coding, because I believe more women in engineering starts with one person saying “you can do this”. Away from the screen: sketching, Carnatic music and a good book.
